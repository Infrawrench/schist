# Public-domain template

Open Publishing Journal Article Template Literary, by Jules (Julian) Luck,
Penn State University Libraries Open Publishing. Published July 3, 2025.

The publisher dedicates this template to the public domain under
[Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The dedication and author are recorded on the [source record](https://zenodo.org/records/15800497);
its API metadata identifies the license as `cc-zero`.

Only the INDD, IDML and reference PDF were acquired on September 30, 2026.
The record's separately listed fonts, font cache and images were not downloaded.
Filenames were shortened; document bytes are unchanged. Source URLs, byte lengths
and SHA-256 digests are in `source.json`. Downloaded files also matched the
publisher's MD5 checksums. The container-only INDD observation is in `probe.json`.
